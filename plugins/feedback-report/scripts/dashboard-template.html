Definitions

FeedbackOne GitHub issue (with its comments), one Discord thread, or one standalone Discord channel post that carries a bug, request, or complaint. Counted once however many messages it holds. Messages by repository collaborators are excluded.
PeopleUnique authors of feedback. People % is the share of all people whose feedback touched an area.
OpenCommunity GitHub issues opened in the window and still open at the cutoff, including issues filed by users' agents. Aging = open for more than two weeks.
Close rateShare of an area's community issues closed by the cutoff.
ResolvedShare of all feedback matched to a closed GitHub issue or a commit on main by the cutoff. GitHub issues use closure data; Discord items were matched by reader agents against commit subjects and issue titles, with every reference validated.
SentimentUnique people per week with a positive or negative record of any kind. Praise share = of people who praised or complained, the share who praised. Plain bug reports are neutral.
SignalA two-period change is called signal only when it exceeds 2·√(prior + latest), the approximate 95% band for a difference of two Poisson counts.
