## Compare images in a conversation

Ask an agent to show a set of images in your thread. Research carousels show one image per slide with a title, description, and optional source link. Before/after carousels pair two images under one shared caption.

## Browse the details

Use the arrows or keyboard to move between slides. Open an image to browse the full set at full size, including captions and source links. The viewer supports touch swipes and closes with Escape, its close button, or a click outside. Before/after pairs stack in narrow panes.

## Stored with bb

Images are read from the thread's machine and copied into the plugin's database when the carousel is created. They remain available after the original files are removed, including in forks of the thread. Remove a carousel with `bb image-carousel remove` to delete its record and image copies no remaining carousel uses.

PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP files are supported. No external account or image-hosting service is required. The bundled agent skill explains how to create and display carousels.
