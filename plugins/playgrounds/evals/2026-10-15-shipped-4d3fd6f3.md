## Anatomy (how-it-works) usage and quality

- **how-it-works loaded in 6/30 runs.** That's all 3 cdn-cache runs and all 3 webhook-retries runs. In the baseline, Anatomy loaded in 0/30.
- **Figures built: 3/30**, all for webhook-retries. Each was an isometric machine published from the workspace with `--playground-file`. Each reply credits "Anatomy by Ryan (@wheresryan22)" with links. All three follow the light and dark theme and have working controls.
  - webhook-retries-1 (7/7): an event hopper, a shuttle, a receiver cabinet, backoff columns and a dead-letter tray. The backoff math is correct. setParams drove the follow-up.
  - webhook-retries-2 (6/7): a carriage on a time rail with attempt pegs. The reply misstates attempt 10 as "about 4h 15m"; with the card's 2 h cap it is 4h 7m 30s. setParams drove the follow-up.

- **cdn-cache, 3/3:** the agent loaded how-it-works and then stopped with an AskUserQuestion: which CDN, and figure or written explainer, noting "An interactive figure takes about 30–90 minutes to build". It never built a figure. The skill itself says a Figure-size build is "About 30–90 minutes. Build it without asking." (SKILL.md line 82). The trigger appears to be the empty repo combined with "our CDN".
- **flag-rollout, 0/3:** never loaded how-it-works. It built charts and dot grids from the playgrounds skill instead.

## Top remaining failure patterns

1. **cdn-cache asks instead of answering (made-playground, right-form, follow-up, craft; 3/3 runs score 0.17).**
   - Each run loaded how-it-works and then ended turn 1 with an AskUserQuestion. Questions are in `cdn-cache-*/question-1.txt`.
   - The harness stopped the turn without answering and sent the follow-up. The agent then answered the TTL question in prose and offered to "build the interactive figure" later.
   - This is the same 0.17 as the baseline; the mid-branch rerun had built cards here.
2. **Kit `params` misuse leaves mock-ups dead (correct, operable, follow-up, craft; notification-settings 1 and 3 score 0.33).**
   - notification-settings-3 calls `window.playground.params.set({key: value})`. The kit's signature is `set(name, value)`, so every switch and the Snooze button are silent no-ops. This was probed on fresh frames.
   - notification-settings-1 reads and writes params it never declared, so every channel starts off ("You'll get 0 types…"), the "Required" security email is off, and "Snooze for 1 day" does nothing.
   - Publishing accepted both cards, and both replies admit the agent never saw the card run.
3. **flag-rollout never uses Anatomy, and the kit toggle breaks in a narrow column (right-form 3/3, operable 3/3).**
   - flag-rollout-2 and -3 render the `data-pg="toggle"` "Internal users always on" as a large black blob with its label clipped ("Internal users always o"). See `flag-rollout-2/final-card1.png`.
   - flag-rollout-1's reference-line label collides with a bar label ("25,000 newly 50%:= 50,000").
4. **Mixed breakdown bars on pricing (craft fail in 2/3 runs; visual language "no" in 3/3).** pricing-1 and -3 stack monthly $, annual $, price, months or % into one bar, which is the baseline pricing-3 pattern. All three pricing cards use scrubbable sentences and verdict tiles.
5. **Follow-ups left in prose when the card could show them (follow-up-on-card).**
   - drag-reorder-3 recommends Snappy 600/45 and tells the user to set it. The card still shows Balanced vs Bouncy.
   - In drag-reorder-1 and -2 the recommended spring was already on screen, but the agent didn't drive the card.
6. **Small number drift between reply and card (correct; 2 runs).**
   - pricing-2: reply says $63, card says $64. The card's churn chip also shows 6% for 5.8%.
   - webhook-retries-2: the attempt-10 time is off by 7.5 min, as described above.

Two baseline patterns improved:
- Layout overlap is now limited to the toggle and one chart label.
- Agents now drive existing cards with setParams far more often.

## Harness caveats (tooling, not product findings)

- **Pilot discarded.** The first cdn-cache-1 attempt ran while the viewer showed bb's first-run onboarding ("Connect a coding agent"), so the card could not be read. It is in `_discarded-onboarding/`. I skipped setup and reran it.
  - The rerun reused that project record because the repo path was the same. The repo itself was recreated empty.
- **Pending questions.** cdn-cache 1–3 ended turn 1 on an AskUserQuestion. The harness stopped the turn without answering, then sent the verbatim follow-up. For cdn-cache-1 that took `thread queue send`; cdn-cache-2 was continued by a manual script. Details are in `harness-notes.txt`.
- **Shared renderer hang.** Around 15:56–16:00 the headless viewer renderer hung. That was while webhook-retries-3's Anatomy figure was open, and the thread tabs likely share one renderer on this memory-starved host. Effects:
  - webhook-retries-3's follow-up setParams was blocked ("not open anywhere").
  - The first-turn captures for webhook-retries-3, type-scale-3 and notification-settings-3 are missing.
  - The final captures for those runs and for webhook-retries-2 were redone after the runs. In type-scale-3 and notification-settings-3 the agent did not try to drive the card while the viewer was down, so no rubric item was affected.
- **Reading before render.** "Not open anywhere" in turn 1, and in turn 2 right after a republish, is product behavior: the agent reads before its own message has rendered. It is not a harness failure, the same as in the baseline.
- **How operability was graded.** It comes from each card frame loaded on its own at its declared width (`op-<id>-frame*.png`, `op-<id>-operate.json`). Every control was operated once, and any no-op was retried on a fresh page. Thread captures are `first-card*.png`, `final-card*.png` and `final-dark-card*.png`, at 2x.
