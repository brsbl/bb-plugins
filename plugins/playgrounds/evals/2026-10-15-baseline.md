# Playgrounds eval: baseline (playgrounds-product-builder-v1)

Build under test: plugin worktree HEAD `1ff4125fe02f0a22360098dbe2be4bfc95d08370` (plugins/playgrounds, bundles `anatomy`), bb core dev app at `1419e3de053d50aa7d84fa3ade8eaf153e7119f3`. Every run used provider `claude-code`, model `claude-opus-5-5`, permission mode `full`, a fresh temp git repo (empty `init` commit) and a new thread. Prompts and follow-ups were sent verbatim.

**Overall mean: 0.76** (passes / applicable items over 30 runs). **Consistency: 6/10 prompts (0.60)** have all three runs within one rubric item of each other.

Scoring notes:
- A run with no card fails made-playground, right-form, operable, follow-up-on-card and craft. `correct` is graded on the prose.
- `blocked` means the agent tried to drive the card (setParams, read or seek) and the eval harness's headless viewer failed. The errors were "not open anywhere", "did not respond in time" or "Available: none". These items are left out of the score. That happened 10 times, all on follow-up-on-card. Counting them as fails lowers the mean to 0.72.
- The 3 cdn-cache runs score 1/6. Without them the mean is 0.83.

## Top 5 failure patterns

1. **Anatomy never used. The anatomy prompts got charts or nothing (right-form, made-playground, credit).** In 0 of 30 runs did the agent load the `anatomy` skill or run its kit. Credit to Ryan (@wheresryan22) was n/a everywhere.
   - All 3 cdn-cache runs answered in prose plus a table and loaded no skill (cdn-cache-1/2/3).
   - All 6 webhook-retries and flag-rollout runs built run-bar simulation charts, e.g. `flag-rollout-1/final-card0.png` and `webhook-retries-2`.
   - The playgrounds skill loaded in 27/30 runs. The agent picked the first matching path and never the isometric figure.
2. **Layout breaks at the card width (operable).**
   - flag-rollout-3: step labels overlap ("10%10%", "50%50%").
   - optimistic-ui-3: the "Tap" and "Request in flight" annotations are drawn on top of each other.
   - webhook-retries-3: the "160 min" and "180 min" axis labels run together.
   - type-scale-3: the table clips its last column ("Updat", "Yesterd").
   - notification-settings-2: labels wrap to "Notification / s", "Snooze / d" and "In- / app".
   - drag-reorder-1: the button wraps to "Shuffl / e".
3. **Reply numbers don't match the card (correct).**
   - drag-reorder-2: the reply says Snappy settles in about 200 ms; the card shows 407 ms and 2.7% overshoot.
   - webhook-retries-1: the reply says 2,090 dead-lettered and a 160/min peak; the card shows 2110 and 120/min.
   - webhook-retries-3: the reply says failed requests decline during the outage; the chart shows them climbing to 120 req/min.
   - release-cadence-2: the reply says 11 days sooner; the card says 12.
   - flag-rollout-2: the card says "our 1,000 customers" while the reply and the card's own result say 1M / 500k.
4. **Kit misuse leaves controls dead or broken (operable, craft).**
   - flag-rollout-2 used `data-suffix`, which the kit ignores (it reads `data-unit`), so every unit disappeared.
   - type-scale-2 put commas inside `data-options` labels, which split one control into 14 bogus segments. The card frame also throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'map')`, and its presets do nothing.
   - drag-reorder-2's preset buttons don't change stiffness or damping.
   - optimistic-ui-1 and drag-reorder-2 ship primary actions as unstyled native browser buttons.
5. **Follow-ups republish a new card instead of driving the existing one, and charts mislead (follow-up-on-card, craft).**
   - 15 of the 20 graded follow-ups passed only by publishing a new card. Only drag-reorder-3 and optimistic-ui-3 changed the existing card with setParams.
   - Every other attempt to drive a card was blocked by the harness, and the agent fell back to prose. Several replies then tell the user "the card isn't open on your screen".
   - pricing-3 and release-cadence-3 stack alternative scenarios, or mixed units (+80 h, -26 h, 13.5 changes), into one breakdown bar.

## Harness caveats (tooling, not product findings)

- **Viewer.** For agents to drive a card, a client has to have it open. I kept each live thread open in a separate headless Chrome viewer, alongside the screenshot Chrome. On this host (swap full, ~1 GB free) the viewer's renderers repeatedly hung or the browser died. That produced the 10 blocked follow-ups.
  - webhook-retries 1-3 ran once with no viewer at all, and pricing-1/2 and optimistic-ui-2 had their follow-ups confounded. I archived all six and reran them. The originals are in `_discarded-noviewer/` and `_discarded-viewer-unavailable/`.
  - The reruns of pricing-1, pricing-2 and optimistic-ui-2 hit the same viewer failure and are graded blocked.
- **funnel-1 follow-up.** One of my edits to the runner script broke it while runs were in flight, and funnel-1's follow-up was never sent. I restored its card state and sent it about an hour later.
- **Thread-UI screenshots.** Captures of the real thread are unreliable here. The thread list re-mounts cards on scroll and they show "Loading playground...", and some captures hung.
  - The first-reply captures missing for optimistic-ui-3 and pricing-1, and the one for cdn-cache-2, could not be redone.
  - Operability and console errors were therefore graded from the card frame loaded on its own at its declared width (`op-<id>-frame.png`, `op-<id>-frame-operated.png`, `op-<id>-operate.json`). Every control was operated there once.
  - A second pass of thread captures is in `final2-*`.
- **optimistic-ui-1 hang.** The thread page for optimistic-ui-1 hung the headless renderer 3 out of 3 times; its card frame loads fine on its own. The cause is unverified.
