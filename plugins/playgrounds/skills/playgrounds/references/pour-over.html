Pour-over coffee

One cup, about four minutes. 20 g coffee to 320 g water.

Heat the water

Bring about 500 g of water to just off the boil. The extra is for rinsing the filter.

Rinse the filter

Fold the paper filter along its seam, set it in the cone, and wet it all over. Pour the rinse water out of the cup.

Add the grounds

Add 20 g of coffee ground medium-fine and shake the cone gently so the bed is flat.

Bloom

Pour about 40 g of water in a spiral to wet every ground, then wait 30 seconds while it bubbles.

Finish the pour

Pour slowly in circles up to 320 g total, keeping the water level steady. It should finish dripping by about 3:30.

Photos from Wikimedia Commons (CC0, public domain, CC BY-SA 3.0). The ratio is 1:16; use 1:15 for stronger coffee.
