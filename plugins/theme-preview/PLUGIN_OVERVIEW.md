## Judge a theme on app surfaces

Open Theme Preview from the sidebar to inspect your palette on a sample bb interface. Switch between Thread, New thread, Split, and Settings views to check the sidebar, conversation, panels, and overlays.

## Compare palettes and modes

The theme picker includes bundled, plugin, and custom themes. Each row previews its own colors and typography. Selecting a row applies the theme and mode to bb itself, including other open windows.

Below the preview, inspect typography, control samples, sidebar states, and resolved surface, text, accent, status, and border values. Text and status samples include contrast ratios against their backgrounds.

## Iterate beside an agent

Keep the preview beside a thread while an agent edits a theme. Saved theme changes refresh the preview and reapply the active palette. The bundled theme-authoring skill explains theme files and tokens.

Requires bb 0.38 or newer. No external account is required; agent-assisted theme editing uses your configured provider.
