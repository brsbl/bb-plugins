## Follow your league

Open Matches to see each Fantasy Premier League Draft matchup, then expand it
for both squads and gameweek points. Live views refresh every minute. The Table
shows results, league points, fantasy points and recent form, with your team
highlighted.

## Plan your waivers

Compare suggested replacements and fallback choices across your full squad.
Expand a claim to compare player statistics, availability and upcoming fixtures.
Waiver order and rival demand are estimates; pending claims are unavailable.
Enter your chosen claims on the Fantasy Premier League Draft website.

Use Ask agent to add the current gameweek or waiver plan to your conversation.
Your existing draft stays in place, and the context resolves when you send.

## Set up

Enter your league ID or league URL in plugin settings, then your team name,
manager name or initials. Both settings start blank. No login, API key or account
connection is required to read a league whose ID you know.

## Data and availability

The plugin reads the public API at
[Fantasy Premier League Draft](https://draft.premierleague.com/). It fetches
league entries and manager names, results, player statistics, squad selections,
player ownership, processed transactions, live points and upcoming fixtures.
Requests run on your bb server; it needs internet access to that service.

The third-party API can be unavailable, delayed or changed without notice.
Future lineups may not be published yet, and live scores can change. A failed
refresh keeps the previous view with a Retry action. Estimates and suggestions
depend on the data available and do not guarantee results.
