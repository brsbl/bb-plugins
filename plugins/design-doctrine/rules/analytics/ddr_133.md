# Make growth dashboards visual first

Growth dashboards should let the reader understand the main result by scanning visuals and short labels in a few seconds.

## Why

The reader needs to recognize results and decide quickly.

## Prefer

- Visualization first
- One headline metric per tab
- Short labels

## Avoid

- Prose-heavy dashboard views

## Use when

- Designing product or growth dashboards and reports

## Do not use when

- Writing detailed methodology or investigative analysis

## Evidence

- The user requested visual-first growth reporting that supports quick scanning with much less prose.

## Check

- Can the main result be identified in a few seconds without reading paragraphs?
