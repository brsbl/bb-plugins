# Preserve the full proposition when emphasizing a headline phrase

In bb awareness headlines, keep the full proposition visually readable as a unit when highlighting a phrase whose meaning depends on the surrounding sentence.

## Why

A highlighted role or outcome can attract attention without communicating what the product does if its explanatory lead-in becomes subordinate.

## Prefer

- Emphasis within a cohesive, readable headline that retains the product and action context.

## Avoid

- Making a context-dependent role noun the dominant standalone message while shrinking its explanatory lead-in.

## Use when

- A bb awareness headline highlights an outcome within a sentence explaining what the IDE can become.

## Do not use when

- The highlighted phrase independently communicates the complete intended message.

## Evidence

- Rejected oversized emphasis on a role noun because it only carried meaning within the full sentence about transforming the IDE.

## Check

- Does the first glance communicate the complete proposition rather than only the highlighted role?
- Is the context needed to interpret the highlighted phrase readily readable?
