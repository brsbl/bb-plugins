# Preserve author-supplied plugin artwork

In plugin discovery, preserve the colors of author-supplied glyphs and images instead of recoloring them to encode category.

## Why

Plugin identity should survive category styling, including custom SVGs added as the catalog grows.

## Prefer

- Treat author artwork separately from generic product icons.
- Choose category treatments that accommodate custom glyphs without recoloring them.

## Avoid

- Applying category ink to author-supplied artwork.
- Assuming future plugin icons will all be generic tintable glyphs.

## Use when

- Styling plugin identity artwork in marketplace cards.

## Do not use when

- Styling generic icons supplied by the host design system.

## Evidence

- During marketplace card iteration, rejected coloring author-supplied glyphs and insisted the treatment accommodate future custom SVGs.

## Check

- Do author-supplied glyphs retain their colors?
- Does the treatment work with custom SVGs as well as generic icons?
