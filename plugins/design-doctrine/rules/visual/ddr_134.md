# Marketing visuals: simple, powerful, logos over words

Public marketing graphics communicate one really simple idea that feels powerful. Build them from bb's real UI or marketing-site components, and use logos and visuals instead of words wherever possible. Avoid dense, generic "AI design" layouts.

## Why

A dense, wordy diagram made of generic cards and pills reads as AI design and hides the point. A simple image built from bb's own components looks native, and a single clear idea feels more powerful than a lot of detail.

## Prefer

- One idea per graphic
- bb UI or getbb.app marketing-site components
- Logos and icons in place of labels
- A handful of words at most

## Avoid

- Generic card-and-pill layouts that read as AI-generated
- Long lists, schedules and paragraphs on an image
- Trying to look crazy or complex to impress

## Use when

- Designing a social post image, launch graphic or explanatory diagram for bb

## Evidence

- Founder rejected a dense, generic-looking org diagram and asked for a simpler graphic built from bb or marketing-site components, with logos and visuals instead of words.

## Check

- Can someone get the idea in 3 seconds?
- Could a logo or visual replace any remaining word?
- Is it built from bb or marketing-site components?
