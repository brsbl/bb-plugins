# Carry bb’s marketing identity into branded stickers

For bb brand-awareness stickers, use the actual bb logo and match the marketing site's visual styling rather than approximating the brand.

## Why

Consistent logo and styling make the printed promotion recognizable as the same product as its marketing site.

## Prefer

- The official bb logo and the marketing site's typography and colors adapted to print.

## Avoid

- Substitute logo treatments or loosely similar typography when representing the existing bb brand.

## Use when

- Designing bb branded stickers intended to carry the marketing site's identity into print.

## Do not use when

- Exploring an explicitly requested new brand direction or a distinct campaign identity.

## Exceptions

- An explicitly requested campaign treatment can depart from the site's styling.

## Evidence

- During sticker design, requested the actual bb logo and exact styling from the marketing site.

## Check

- Does the sticker use the actual bb logo?
- Does its typography and color treatment match the marketing site's identity?
