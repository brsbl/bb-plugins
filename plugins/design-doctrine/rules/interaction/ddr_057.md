# Center mobile toasts below app navigation

In bb mobile layouts, place toast notifications at the top center, below the app navigation.

## Why

This placement follows the requested mobile notification hierarchy and leaves the top navigation visible.

## Prefer

- Horizontally centered toasts beneath the top navigation.

## Avoid

- Positioning mobile toasts over the top navigation.

## Use when

- Positioning toast notifications in bb mobile layouts.

## Do not use when

- Positioning desktop notifications or notifications in other products.

## Evidence

- In a mobile toast placement episode, the user requested top-center positioning and then specified placement below the app navigation.

## Check

- Are mobile toasts horizontally centered below the top navigation?
