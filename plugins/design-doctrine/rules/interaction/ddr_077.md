# Use the whole card for simple disclosure

In the FPL Draft plugin, let simple match rows and waiver cards expand by clicking the whole surface, with a pointer cursor and without a separate disclosure caret.

## Why

A single large interaction target keeps repeated cards compact and removes redundant disclosure controls.

## Prefer

- Make the full card or row the expansion target.
- Use a pointer cursor to signal clickability.

## Avoid

- Separate carets for these simple expandable cards.
- Limiting expansion to a small region of the card.

## Use when

- Designing simple expandable match rows or waiver cards in the FPL Draft plugin.

## Do not use when

- Designing unrelated navigation trees or complex cards with competing actions.

## Exceptions

- Preserve keyboard access and semantic expanded state; the visual preference does not remove accessible disclosure behavior.

## Evidence

- During a plugin refinement, the user requested whole-card expansion and removal of carets from both waiver cards and match rows, using a pointer cursor.

## Check

- Does clicking the card surface expand its details?
- Are redundant disclosure carets absent?
- Is the interactive surface indicated with a pointer cursor?
