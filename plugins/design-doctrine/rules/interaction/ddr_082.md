# Stop panel resizing at its limits during the drag

In BB, constrain the right-panel divider to its allowed size range throughout dragging, so releasing it does not snap an out-of-bounds preview back to a limit.

## Why

The visible position during a resize should accurately show the size the user can keep on release.

## Prefer

- Stop the divider at both size limits while the pointer is still down.
- Keep the boundary stable when releasing a drag at either limit.

## Avoid

- Allowing the panel to visibly exceed its bounds and correcting it only on release.

## Use when

- Designing or changing the bounded resize interaction for the BB thread right panel.

## Do not use when

- Designing scrolling, freeform dragging, or another gesture with intentionally elastic feedback.

## Evidence

- Rejected right-panel overshoot followed by snap-back and asked to preserve stopping at both limits during dragging.

## Check

- Does the divider stop at each limit before release?
- Does releasing beyond either limit leave the boundary in its displayed position?
