# Keep reasoning effort directly selectable

In BB’s model picker, prefer a compact single row of reasoning levels that can be changed directly without opening another control.

## Why

The user valued the simplest incremental improvement and avoiding an extra click to change reasoning.

## Prefer

- Keep supported reasoning choices visible and directly selectable.
- Keep the reasoning section compact within the model menu.

## Avoid

- Moving routine reasoning selection behind an edit panel or submenu solely to reorganize the picker.

## Use when

- Designing BB’s model-picker reasoning controls when the supported choices fit legibly.

## Do not use when

- The available space or number of choices makes a single row unusable.

## Exceptions

- Preserve legibility and accessible targets when space prevents the preferred layout.

## Evidence

- Chose a single row because it was simplest, incremental, and avoided an extra click to change reasoning.

## Check

- Can the user change reasoning directly from the open model picker?
- Does the row fit without hiding labels or making targets unusable?
