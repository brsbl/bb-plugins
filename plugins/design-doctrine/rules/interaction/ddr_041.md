# Make annotation order match the visual tour

Number annotations sequentially in their visible page order and provide previous and next navigation through the same sequence.

## Why

A mismatched sequence makes guided pages difficult to scan and traverse.

## Prefer

- Consistent visible and navigable numbering per page.

## Avoid

- Numbering that jumps around the page or disagrees with navigation.

## Use when

- Designing or revising the bb Plugin Guide and its capability examples.

## Do not use when

- Unrelated product layouts or technical API reference documentation.

## Evidence

- Asked to reorder hard-to-follow annotations and add forward and backward card navigation.
- Later again rejected out-of-order numbers and requested sequential annotations on all pages.

## Check

- Does stepping through every annotation follow its visible numbering?
