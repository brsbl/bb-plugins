# Keep onboarding continuation actions near their controls

Place onboarding continuation actions close to the controls they apply to, rather than separating them with a large empty area in a viewport-bottom footer.

## Why

The user finds Continue far below the related controls unergonomic.

## Prefer

- Content-adjacent continuation actions

## Avoid

- Large empty gaps between setup controls and Continue

## Use when

- Designing or reviewing bb onboarding layouts

## Evidence

- User requested content-adjacent onboarding continuation actions as part of the layout proposal.

## Check

- On short onboarding screens, is Continue near the related controls?
