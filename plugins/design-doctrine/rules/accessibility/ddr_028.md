# Make hover-revealed controls discoverable and operable

When controls or content appear on hover, leave a quiet at-rest cue for where they live, reveal them on keyboard focus too, and keep them operable without precise pointing; users must not memorize invisible hit areas.

## Why

Progressive disclosure should reduce clutter without making the affordance impossible to locate or reachable only by mouse.

## Prefer

- Leave a quiet positional cue at rest.
- Reveal the control on hover and focus, with an accessible name and concise tooltip.
- Keep revealed content dismissible, hoverable, and present until the user moves on.

## Avoid

- Completely invisible hit areas.
- Hover-only access with no keyboard path.
- Revealed targets too small or crowded to hit reliably.

## Use when

- A control or supplementary content is hidden until hover to reduce density.

## Do not use when

- The control is always visible or the entire containing object is the target.

## Evidence

- Requested a persistent drag-handle cue and accurate tooltip so its location is not guesswork before hover.

## Check

- Can a user locate the affordance before hovering precisely?
- Does keyboard focus reveal and operate it?
- Is the tooltip supplemental rather than essential?
- Can hover or focus content be dismissed without moving the pointer or focus, hovered without vanishing, and does it persist until dismissed (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.13)?
- Is each revealed target at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels or adequately spaced (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8)?
