## Pick where you type

Type `:` in the composer and pause for 250 ms, then click an emoji. Continuing to type or leaving the composer before the picker opens cancels it. It replaces the colon and keeps the rest of your message. Dismiss the picker to keep the colon as punctuation; choosing an emoji never sends the message.

On desktop, the picker opens above the colon, or below when space is tight. On mobile, it opens in a drawer.

## Find your emoji

Search names, keywords, shortcodes, or an emoji, or browse eight categories. Use arrow keys to browse and click to insert. Enter or Space dismisses the picker and returns to your draft without selecting an emoji or sending a message. Your browser remembers your skin tone and last 24 choices.

## System emojis, offline

The picker displays Unicode emojis using your operating system's emoji font. Appearance and supported emojis vary by system. The emoji dataset is bundled; no account or external service is needed, and searches stay on your device.
