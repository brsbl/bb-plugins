# Keep disclosure spacing outside provider tiles

In bb's mobile Recent list, keep the disclosure caret beside the provider tile rather than reserving an empty caret slot inside tiles on rows without children.

## Why

Empty disclosure slots were rejected as visually poor; aligning icons should not come at the expense of the provider tile's composition.

## Prefer

- Place the caret to the left, outside the provider tile, with a small visible gap.
- Keep tiles without disclosure controls free of empty internal caret slots.

## Avoid

- Reserving blank space inside provider tiles solely to match the caret position on parent rows.

## Use when

- Arranging provider tiles and disclosure carets in the mobile Recent thread list.

## Do not use when

- Reserving space outside a tile to preserve hierarchy indentation.
- Reserving space for a transient control to prevent hover reflow.

## Evidence

- Rejected empty icon spacing in mobile Recent rows and requested the caret outside the provider tile on its left.

## Check

- Do rows without children avoid blank caret slots inside their provider tiles?
- Does the caret sit outside the provider tile with visible separation?
