# Truncate pill labels while preserving the pill shape

In bb thread titles, ellipsize the text inside mention pills while preserving their complete fill, border, padding, and rounded shape; use consistent truncation behavior in sidebar and header titles.

## Why

A constrained title should retain recognizable pill boundaries and behave consistently wherever the same title appears.

## Prefer

- Ellipsis within the pill label.
- Complete pill styling at narrow widths.
- Consistent overflow behavior across title surfaces.

## Avoid

- Clipping pill borders or rounded edges.
- Allowing pills to overflow their title container.
- Different truncation behavior between sidebar and header titles.

## Use when

- Rendering mention pills in width-constrained bb thread titles.

## Do not use when

- Designing unrelated content overflow or intentionally different components.

## Evidence

- The user requested text-only pill truncation with intact fill, border, padding, and rounded shape, then explicitly requested matching header and sidebar behavior.

## Check

- With long labels at narrow and normal widths, does only the pill text ellipsize while its complete shape remains visible?
- Do sidebar, regular-header, and split-pane titles use consistent truncation behavior?
