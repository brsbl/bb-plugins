# Keep follow-up labels tertiary and counts subordinate

In the follow-ups section, keep the section label and its count at tertiary emphasis, with the count slightly subordinate to the label while remaining legible.

## Why

Section metadata should provide orientation without competing with message content; its internal hierarchy should remain readable.

## Prefer

- Quiet tertiary emphasis for the follow-ups label and count.
- A count slightly less prominent than its label while still easy to read.

## Avoid

- A visually loud follow-ups label.
- A count that competes with its label or becomes difficult to read.

## Use when

- Styling the follow-ups section label and adjacent count.

## Do not use when

- Designing primary headings, message content, or attention-demanding status indicators.

## Evidence

- In a follow-ups typography refinement, the user rejected a loud tertiary label and requested a slightly subordinate but legible count.

## Check

- Do the label and count both read as tertiary metadata?
- Is the count slightly subordinate to the label without sacrificing legibility?
