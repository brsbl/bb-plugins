# Use restrained selection emphasis in the model picker

Prefer restrained neutral selection styling for BB’s reasoning controls, with model checkmarks using a consistent selection treatment.

## Why

The user found primary-color emphasis on selected reasoning too heavy and requested matching checkmark color.

## Prefer

- Subtle selection emphasis consistent across the model and reasoning sections.

## Avoid

- Primary-color emphasis that gives routine reasoning selection excessive visual weight.

## Use when

- Styling selection states in BB’s model and reasoning picker.

## Do not use when

- Styling primary actions or semantic status indicators outside this picker.

## Exceptions

- Maintain sufficient contrast and a distinguishable selected state.

## Evidence

- Found primary-color reasoning selection too heavy and asked for model checkmarks to match reasoning selection.

## Check

- Is selection clear without dominating the menu?
- Are model and reasoning selection colors consistent?
