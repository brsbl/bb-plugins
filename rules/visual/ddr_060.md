# Keep guide badges clear of active annotated content

Keep Plugin Guide annotation badges visible and separated from one another and from the foreground controls or labels they explain; overlap with background content behind an overlay can be acceptable.

## Why

Badges should clarify the visible interface without hiding its subject or becoming indistinguishable from each other.

## Prefer

- Separate badges and readable foreground targets.
- Judge overlap in the context of the visible overlay layer.

## Avoid

- Badge collisions.
- Badges covering the foreground control they annotate.
- Treating obscured background content as equivalent to the active overlay content.

## Use when

- Placing annotation badges on Plugin Guide fixtures.

## Do not use when

- Ordinary unannotated interfaces.

## Exceptions

- A badge beside an overlay may overlap content behind that overlay.

## Evidence

- Flagged colliding badges and a covered header control while accepting overlap with a label behind an overlay.

## Check

- Are badges separated and their foreground targets readable?
- Is any accepted content overlap confined to the background behind an overlay?
