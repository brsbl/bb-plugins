# Fit the fixture while scrolling only the page list

Make Plugin Guide fixtures responsive to the window while allowing the horizontal page list to scroll independently; use additional room on larger displays and keep the fixture and card together where practical.

## Why

The guide should remain readable and complete without clipping or unnecessary scrolling.

## Prefer

- Responsive fixtures with coordinated annotations.
- Horizontal overflow confined to the page list at narrow widths.
- Larger presentation when more space is available.

## Avoid

- Applying page-list scrolling to the fixture.
- Excessive spacing that pushes the card below the viewport.

## Use when

- Designing or revising the bb Plugin Guide and its capability examples.

## Do not use when

- Unrelated product layouts or technical API reference documentation.

## Exceptions

- The page list may scroll horizontally when its items exceed the available width.

## Evidence

- Repeatedly clarified that the horizontal page list should scroll while the fixture should be responsive.
- Requested above-the-fold presentation, larger sizing on larger displays, and less fixture-to-card spacing.

## Check

- Does the page list scroll at narrow widths without clipping the fixture?
- Does the fixture use additional space on larger displays?
