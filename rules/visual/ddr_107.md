# Inline bb wordmarks should be peers of headline text

When the bb wordmark replaces a word within a headline, size and position it to read as a visual peer of the surrounding words.

## Why

A wordmark used within a sentence needs a coherent visual relationship with its neighboring words for the heading to read as one phrase.

## Prefer

- Optical sizing and placement that give the inline wordmark equal visual standing with adjacent headline words.

## Avoid

- An inline wordmark that appears oversized, undersized, or vertically detached from the surrounding phrase.

## Use when

- The bb wordmark is embedded as a word in a page headline.

## Do not use when

- The logo is a standalone brand mark or navigation identity.

## Evidence

- In a marketplace heading revision, the user requested that the bb wordmark sizing and placement make it look like a peer to the surrounding words.

## Check

- Does the inline wordmark read as a peer of adjacent words in size and vertical placement?
