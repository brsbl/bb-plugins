# Generative visuals read as one cohesive visual system

In generative or motion demo visuals, make geometric styles, textures, and colors feel like members of one system rather than a collage of unrelated treatments; let variety come from variation within shared rules.

## Why

Mixed styles and palettes that do not share a system make a piece feel incoherent and undercut its message.

## Prefer

- A shared palette and stroke or geometry vocabulary across all scene elements
- Distinct elements that differ along deliberate axes of one system

## Avoid

- Stacking unrelated geometric styles or color schemes in one piece
- Adding variety by importing a new visual language per element

## Use when

- Designing generative animation, demo films, or promo visuals with multiple element types

## Do not use when

- The brief explicitly asks for deliberate clash or collage

## Evidence

- User rejected a generative demo because its geometric styles and colors did not feel like a cohesive system

## Check

- Can every element be described by the same small set of style rules and palette?
