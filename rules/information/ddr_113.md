# Keep thread relationship details in Info and navigation compact

In bb, keep passive thread relationship metadata in Thread info, with a compact direct shortcut for navigating related threads instead of relationship pills or banners in the header and above the composer.

## Why

Relationship details should remain accessible without consuming routine conversation space or forcing users through an information page just to navigate.

## Prefer

- Keep relationship facts together in Thread info.
- Provide a concise related-thread menu that navigates directly to its destinations.
- Use clear sections and progressive disclosure for secondary Info details.

## Avoid

- Passive relationship banners above the prompt.
- Relationship status pills or tags in the thread header.
- Requiring users to open Thread info for every related-thread navigation.

## Use when

- Designing how bb exposes thread relationships and navigation between related threads.

## Do not use when

- Designing unrelated metadata surfaces or deciding agent supervision and notification policy.

## Exceptions

- This concerns passive relationship metadata; actionable approvals and questions are a separate interaction.

## Evidence

- During relationship redesign, the user moved relationship facts into Info, rejected composer banners and header tags, and requested compact direct navigation.

## Check

- Are passive relationship facts concentrated in Thread info?
- Can users navigate directly to related threads through a compact affordance?
- Does the header and composer avoid repeating relationship metadata?
