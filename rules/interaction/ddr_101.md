# Bound inline documents and open long content in a tab

Limit the height of inline documents; when content is truncated, make the preview itself open the full document tab for editing.

## Why

Long documents should transition to a larger editing surface without overwhelming the conversation.

## Prefer

- A bounded inline preview for long documents.
- Clicking the truncated preview to open the document tab.

## Avoid

- Unlimited inline document height.
- A separate Edit in tab button inside the truncated preview.

## Use when

- Embedding editable documents in a conversational timeline with a full document tab available.

## Do not use when

- The full document workspace itself.
- Surfaces without an available full editor.

## Evidence

- During inline document refinement, the user requested a maximum height and replaced the truncation button with clicking the preview to open the tab.

## Check

- Does long content stop expanding the timeline?
- Does activating the truncated preview open the full document for editing?
