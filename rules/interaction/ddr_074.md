# Dismiss completion notifications after arrival

In bb mobile, dismiss a thread-completion notification shortly after its Open action has fully navigated to the destination, keeping it visible during loading.

## Why

The dismissal should follow arrival at the destination while avoiding a lingering notification once the user gets there.

## Prefer

- A brief pause after the destination is fully visible, followed by dismissal.

## Avoid

- Starting the dismissal delay when navigation merely begins.
- A half-second pause after arrival.

## Use when

- Designing the Open action on bb mobile thread-completion notifications.

## Do not use when

- Choosing general animation durations or operating-system notification behavior.

## Evidence

- In a completion-notification episode, the user requested a small dismissal delay, said half a second felt long, and required fully completed navigation first.

## Check

- Does the notification remain during destination loading?
- Does dismissal follow a brief pause after arrival?
