# Keep timeline image navigation within the disclosed sequence

Navigate timeline images in sequential order within the currently loaded timeline and its expanded content. Exclude images in collapsed sections, including incomplete turns, and stop at each end instead of cycling.

## Why

The lightbox should follow the conversation the user has exposed, preserving its order and boundaries.

## Prefer

- Previous and next navigation through eligible inline and table images in timeline order.
- Gallery membership that respects pagination and expansion state.
- Navigation that stops at the first and last eligible image.

## Avoid

- Revealing collapsed images through gallery navigation.
- Including unloaded timeline history.
- Wrapping from the final image to the first or vice versa.

## Use when

- Designing navigation across images in a bb conversation timeline.

## Do not use when

- Designing a separate full-history media browser.
- Designing unrelated carousels.

## Exceptions

- An image already being viewed remains visible if it later becomes ineligible; this does not retain other navigable images.

## Evidence

- In timeline lightbox feedback, the user requested sequential arrows, limited membership to the current paginated and expanded timeline, and rejected cycling at the ends.

## Check

- Does navigation preserve timeline order across messages and tables?
- Are collapsed and unloaded images excluded?
- Does navigation stop at both ends?
