# Preserve sidebar collapse choices until navigation or new unread activity

In bb, preserve manually collapsed sidebar sections when reopening the sidebar or updating the currently open thread. Expand a section when the user navigates to a thread inside it, or when a thread that is not currently open becomes unread.

## Why

Manual collapse expresses the user's preferred navigation state; explicit navigation and new unread activity elsewhere provide reasons to reveal a section.

## Prefer

- Preserve collapsed sections across mobile sidebar closing and reopening.
- Keep the open thread's section collapsed until the user navigates away and back.
- Reveal a collapsed section when another thread within it becomes unread.

## Avoid

- Expanding a section merely because it contains the currently open thread.
- Resetting section collapse choices when reopening the mobile sidebar.

## Use when

- Designing bb sidebar section expansion in response to navigation, sidebar reopening, or thread unread changes.

## Do not use when

- Defining disclosure behavior for unrelated accordions or content panels.

## Exceptions

- Navigation to a thread inside a collapsed section reveals that section.
- A thread becoming unread while it is not currently open reveals its section.

## Evidence

- The user asked for sticky sidebar collapse, specifying navigation back to the open thread and newly unread activity in another thread as reveal triggers.

## Check

- Does collapsing the open thread's section survive closing and reopening the mobile sidebar?
- Does the section stay collapsed while the user remains on that thread?
- Does navigating away and back reveal the thread's section?
- Does another thread becoming unread reveal its collapsed section?
