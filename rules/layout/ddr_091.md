# Keep compact tab groups left-aligned without stretching to fill space

In bb compact tab headers, keep tabs and their navigation controls grouped and left-aligned. Size short tabs to their content, truncate overflow, and use wider headers to expose more tabs instead of stretching a single tab or distributing excess space between controls.

## Why

Excess space inside short tabs or between related controls weakens grouping and can hide useful navigation despite available width.

## Prefer

- Use consistent internal padding and compact spacing between related controls.
- Keep the order previous arrow, tabs, next arrow, add.
- Allow additional tabs at wider widths while keeping spare space after the group.

## Avoid

- Stretching short tab titles into oversized slots.
- Spreading controls across the header solely to fill its width.
- Keeping only one tab visible at wider widths when more can fit.

## Use when

- Laying out bb tab navigation at compact and medium widths.

## Do not use when

- Setting a universal density rule for unrelated toolbars or desktop panes.

## Exceptions

- Leave enough separation for reliable interaction; compact grouping does not mean eliminating all padding.

## Evidence

- During a responsive tab redesign, the user rejected oversized short tabs and asked for left-aligned tabs and controls without unnecessary width filling.

## Check

- Do short titles avoid oversized slots?
- Does a wider compact header expose more tabs?
- Does spare space follow the navigation group instead of separating its controls?
