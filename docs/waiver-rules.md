# Waiver evidence rules

Recommendations are deterministic. A candidate must first improve the existing
points/minutes/availability/fixture rating by more than 0.15. The rules then
assess every held player in that position against the shortlisted free players.
Supported upgrades come before depth options; the existing rating gain breaks
ties. Each position contributes one swap, with up to two fallbacks that pass
the same rules for the same outgoing player.

| Evidence | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Established playing time | At least 180 minutes, two starts and 60 minutes per game. An increase of at least 20 minutes per game supports an upgrade from a fringe slot. Replacing a fit player already averaging 30+ minutes requires another supporting signal. |
| Availability | A fully available player with established playing time can replace an injured, suspended, doubtful or unavailable player. |
| Attacking involvement | At least 0.10 more xG+xA per 90, with 180 minutes and two starts. Divide by at least 180 minutes to limit cameo inflation. |
| Defensive involvement | At least two more defensive contributions per 90, reaching 10 for defenders or 12 for midfielders/forwards. This is supporting evidence, not a prediction of match-level DC points. |
| Form and fixtures | At least 0.5 higher published Form and an average FDR improvement of 2/3 across three fixtures, with at least 450 minutes and five starts. Goals substantially ahead of xG block this route. |
| Depth | A limited-playing-time player can be replaced by someone with at least 20 more minutes per game, 90 total minutes and one start. A covered backup-keeper upgrade is also depth. |

Attacking, defensive and Form evidence cannot justify losing more than 20 minutes
per game. Incoming players without established playing time remain depth options.
All 15 squad members stay eligible; the selected starting XI does not gate swaps.

The assessment records missing data, small samples, harder fixtures, short
clean-sheet streaks, lower attacking involvement and goals substantially ahead of xG. Unknown values never
become zero. The card shows a short classification; Ask agent receives the
programmatic evidence and cautions without adding prose to the cards.

The DC thresholds follow [FPL's scoring rules](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2174909).
The other thresholds are explicit policy choices, not backtested probabilities or predicted
points. The existing shortlist is bounded to six free players per position by
the legacy points/minutes rating. Minutes, starts and xG/xA use season totals;
Form uses FPL's published recent value. The rules cannot infer future team
selection, tactical roles or unreported injuries.
